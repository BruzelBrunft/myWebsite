2. Description of the Service

2.1 What SaferChatAI Does

SaferChatAI is a Discord bot that supports content moderation on Discord servers. The Bot uses artificial intelligence (AI) to analyze messages and identify potentially problematic content that may violate community guidelines.

The Service includes:

  • AI-powered analysis of message content to detect toxicity, hate speech, self-harm language, NSFW content, spam, and piracy
  • Context-aware assessment considering conversation history
  • Reporting potentially problematic content to the server's moderation team
  • Providing a moderation dashboard with statistics and analytics
  • Ability to mark false positives to improve AI accuracy
  • Automatic notification when self-harm language is detected, with crisis resources

2.2 No Automatic Moderation by Default

By default, the Bot does not make automatic moderation decisions such as deleting messages, issuing warnings, or imposing timeouts or bans. The Bot reports potentially problematic content to the moderation team, which then conducts human review and makes decisions.

Optional auto-delete: Server administrators can optionally enable automatic deletion of flagged messages. This is not the default setting and must be explicitly activated.

2.3 Beta Status

The Service is currently in beta phase. This means features may not be fully developed, errors can occur, and changes to functionality may be made. We strive for maximum stability and quality but cannot guarantee error-free operation.
