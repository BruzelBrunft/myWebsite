Questions

The things you're probably thinking.

Run /config in any channel the bot can see. A visual panel opens — pick your mod channel, optionally mark exempt channels, and you're done. Monitoring starts immediately.

SaferChatAI doesn't auto-delete by default. It reports to your mod channel and your team reviews with one tap. Hit "False Positive" and it learns — accuracy improves over time.

Read Messages, Send Messages, Manage Messages, and Moderate Members. SaferChatAI will never auto-ban and never reads DMs.

Messages are sent to a third-party AI provider for analysis — API data deleted within 30 days, not used for training. Only flagged messages are stored. Normal conversation is never saved. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

SaferChatAI runs alongside your existing setup as a second layer. It catches what keyword filters miss: harassment that "technically didn't break any rules", coded hate speech, context-dependent toxicity. Think of it as the judgment layer on top.
